Our Story

From a night train to 98k travellers a year

Stackly began in 2011 when our founders missed a connection in Zurich and discovered the best view of their lives by accident. Today we're a 120-person team across four time zones, obsessed with one thing: removing every ounce of friction between you and the journey.

We partner directly with 900+ airlines, hotel groups and local guides, and our pricing engine scans fares every 15 minutes. But the tech is only half of it — every itinerary is sanity-checked by a human who has actually been there.
